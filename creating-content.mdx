## Linking Content

In Kith, you can link Google Docs, Word documents, and more to your Knowledge Base.

<Note>
  In order to do this, you must have the following permissions: **View Knowledge** and **Update Knowledge**
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your Knowledge Base">
    Click Knowledge Base in the sidebar and then click Categories or stay on the homepage if the category you want to add content to is pinned to the homescreen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Getting to the Add Content Menu">
    Find the category you want to add your content to and click **Add Content**. This will bring up a document editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your Content!">
    Click Link to Doc and then enter your document's URL and a name and icon
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Making Content

In Kith, there is a richly featured Document Editor to create your team's one unified Knowledge Base.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your Knowledge Base">
    Click Knowledge Base in the sidebar and then click Categories or stay on the homepage if the category you want to add content to is pinned to the homescreen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Getting to the Document Editor">
    Find the category you want to add your content to and click **Add Content**. This will bring up the Document Editor
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your content!">
    In the Document Editor there are lots of options to make your document truely **_yours_**. After making your document your done!

    <Frame>
      ![Creating Content Middle Image](/images/Creating-Content-Middle-Image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Composed Files vs. Linked Files

A document made in the Document Editor is stored on Kith's servers, whereas a Linked File is stored on a third-party server, such as Google Docs or YouTube.
