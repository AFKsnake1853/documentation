## Viewing your Activity

You can view your Activity by going to [https://app.prismspace.xyz](https://app.prismspace.xyz) and clicking Activity in the sidebar.

## Installing the Module

Our Activity Tracking module can be installed with a few simple steps. The system will automatically track when your team join and leave your game.

<Tip>
  Before you continue, make sure you have [enabled the HttpService](http://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/HttpService) in your game's security settings.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  All our scripts are obfuscated to protect Kith scripts from being copied by others. This includes the Activity Tracking module. If you are not comfortable with this, please contact us so we can sort out a solution.
</Warning>
