## Joining Sessions

Joining Sessions allow you to reserver your spot on the session and automatically logs your session for your assignments!

<Callout icon="info" iconType="solid" color="#3B82F6">
  For claiming Host and Co-Host role, be sure that you have the following permission: **Assign Host to Self** (for Hosts), **Assign Co-Host to Self** (for Co-Hosts).
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Session Calendar">
    To first join a session, first go to the session calendar by going to Activity → Sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Session">
    In the Session Calendar, select a session that you want to join.

    Please note that you can also switch the day that you want to view by clicking the week above
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the Session!">
    In the self-signup popout window, select the role you want and hit **Claim it** to take the role.

    <Warning>
      **Can’t see any servers?** That may mean that this session doesn’t have any servers as of now. Check back later if there’s one available.
    </Warning>

    <Frame>
      ![Joining Sessions Bottom Image](/images/Joining-Sessions-Bottom-Image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
