---
title: "Retrieve Active Sessions"
api: "GET https://api.lumonol.co.uk/sessions"
description: "Retrieve a list of all active or scheduled sessions for your workspace."
---

<ParamField query="workspace_id" type="string" required>
  Your unique Workspace ID.
</ParamField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X GET "https://api.lumonol.co.uk/sessions?workspace_id=YOUR_WORKSPACE_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
