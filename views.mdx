## Creating Views

Views are the one-stop shop in Kith to retrieve insights in seconds. Allowing for effortless staff management.

Heres how to create a view:

<Warning>
  Before you continue, make sure you have the **Create Views** permission in your Kith workspace.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Getting to Staff Management">
    In your Kith workspace, click Staff Management in the sidebar and you will be taken to the Staff Management Dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Opening the Create a View window">
    Click the plus button next to views to open the create a view menu. This is where you will configure your view. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your view!">
    You can cutomise your view to be truely yours. From name to icon to filters your view will actually be yours.

## Filtering Staff

Filter allows you to filter staff that doesn't fit any of the requirements you've set. You can do this to filter which staff are at a certain rank, has a certain department and many more!

To do this, click **Filter** and then click **Add Filter**, select a property that you would like to filter by, select from the dropdown the filter type and it's value.

<img
  src="/images/FilterView.png"

## Sorting Staff

Sorting staff allows you to sort your staff, this neatly organises them by rank, department, and more.

To do this, click Sort and click Add Sort, same as filter, select a property that you eant to sort out, and select if the sort is accending <Icon icon="arrow-up" /> or decending <Icon icon="arrow-down" />.

## Hiding Fields

When creating new views, all fields will be shown by default. But sometimes this can cause your view to feel cluttered with information you might not need. Hiding FIelds solves this issue.

Hiding Fields allows you to select ehich fields will only be shown to the view. To do this, click **Hide** and check which fields will be shown or not.
