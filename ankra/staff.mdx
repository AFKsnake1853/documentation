The **Staff** module lists everyone in the ranks you switched on in **Settings › Activity Tracking**. People appear whether or not they have signed in to Ankra.

## Dashboard

**Staff › Dashboard** shows headline numbers for the week and your top performers.

## Staff profiles

Click anyone to open their profile. Its tabs are:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Details** | Their rank, role, department and directory details. |
| **Activity** | Minutes and messages in game. |
| **Sessions** | Sessions they hosted or attended. |
| **Logbook** | Their record. See [Logbook](/ankra/logbook). |
| **Assignments** | This week's assignments and their progress. |
| **Time off** | Their requests. See [Time off](/ankra/time-off). |

A tab only appears when its module is on and your role can see it.

## Timesheets

**Staff › Timesheets** shows each person's activity hour by hour for a day. Managers can add a record for time the game didn't report.

## Team views

A team view is a saved roster: a set of filters, sorts and columns. Share one with the team or keep it to yourself.

## Labels

Labels mark people, such as **Trainee** or **On probation**. Create them in **Settings › Labels**, then add them to people from the **+** above their name on a team view's card.
