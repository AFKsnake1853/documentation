## Request time off

Open **Activity › Inactivity** and fill in the form: your dates, the kind of time off, and any notes. Your request is pending until a manager reviews it.

## Review requests

Managers see pending requests in **Staff › Time off**, and each person's on the **Time off** tab of their profile. Approving or denying needs the **Approve time off** permission.

A manager can also add time off for someone from their profile. It's added as already approved.

## Settings

**Settings › Inactivity** sets the questions the form asks, and its policies.
