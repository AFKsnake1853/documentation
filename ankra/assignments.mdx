Assignments are weekly targets. Each one applies to the roles you choose and resets every week.

## Kinds of assignment

| Kind | Done when |
|---|---|
| **Checkbox Assignment** | It's ticked off. Choose whether people can tick their own. |
| **Minutes in Game** | They reach the target minutes in game. |
| **Sessions Attended** | They attend the target number of sessions. |
| **Sessions Hosted** | They host the target number of sessions. |

A target of `0` still tracks progress, but leaves the assignment off the person's list of things to do.

## Where to find them

- **Assignments › Assigned** shows this week's assignments.
- **Assignments › History** shows earlier weeks.

Create and edit assignments in **Settings › Assignments**.
