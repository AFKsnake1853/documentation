---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Connect your Roblox group, choose who counts as staff, and start tracking activity."
icon: "rocket"
---

You need to be a workspace admin for every step here. Everything is under **Settings**, at the bottom of the sidebar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Roblox group">
    Open **Settings › General** and enter your **Roblox group ID**. It's the number in your group's address on Roblox: `roblox.com/communities/`**`123456`**`/…`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who counts as staff">
    Open **Settings › Activity Tracking** and switch on each rank that is staff. Everyone in those ranks appears on the roster, and their time in game is recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up roles">
    Open **Settings › Roles** to decide what each role can see and do, such as who can write in the logbook or approve time off. See [Roles and permissions](/ankra/roles-and-permissions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the activity loader to your game">
    On **Settings › Activity Tracking**, generate the loader and download it. Put the script in **ServerScriptService**, then turn on **Game Settings › Security › Allow HTTP Requests** in Roblox Studio. See [Activity](/ankra/activity#install-the-activity-loader).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Auto Ranking (optional)">
    To promote and demote people from Ankra, open **Settings › Auto Ranking › Configuration** and sign in with a Roblox account that can change ranks in your group. See [Auto Ranking](/ankra/auto-ranking).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Activity isn't recorded in Roblox Studio play tests, so your own testing doesn't show up on the leaderboard. Join the live game to check it works.
</Tip>
