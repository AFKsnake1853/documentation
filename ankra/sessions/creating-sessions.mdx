Sessions live in the **Activity** module, under **Activity › Sessions**.

A session type is a repeating event, such as **Training** every Saturday at 7 PM. Create one with a name, its first date, a start time, and how often it repeats: once, daily, weekly, every two weeks, or monthly.

<Note>
  A monthly session keeps its first day of the month. One that starts on the 31st only happens in months with a 31st.
</Note>

## Ad-hoc sessions

A session type can allow ad-hoc sessions: one started on the spot, on a day it doesn't normally run. Turn this on when editing the session type.
