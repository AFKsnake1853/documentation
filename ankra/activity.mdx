The **Activity** module records how long your staff spend in game. Your game reports it through the activity loader, a script you add once.

## History and leaderboard

- **Activity › History** shows your own minutes, week by week.
- **Activity › Leaderboard** ranks everyone by minutes.

## Who is tracked

Only people in the ranks switched on in **Settings › Activity Tracking** are recorded. The loader reports everyone in the server, and Ankra keeps the ranks you chose. Change the ranks at any time without touching your game.

## Install the activity loader

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate it">
    In **Settings › Activity Tracking**, click **Generate loader**, then **Download loader**. The script already contains your workspace and a key, named **Activity loader** in **Settings › Developer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your game">
    In Roblox Studio, put the script in **ServerScriptService**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow HTTP requests">
    Turn on **Game Settings › Security › Allow HTTP Requests**, then publish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The loader reads its settings from Ankra each time a server starts, so you never need to replace it when settings change.

<Warning>
  The loader contains an API key. Keep the script private. Anyone holding the key can report activity for your workspace.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The loader also enforces Lumonol's platform-wide blacklist. Banned users, and members of blacklisted groups, are removed from your game.
</Note>
