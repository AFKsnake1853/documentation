The **Knowledge** module is your team's handbook.

- **Knowledge › Home** shows featured categories and quick links.
- **Knowledge › Categories** lists every category and its documents.

A category can be limited to certain roles. A document can be a page written in Ankra, or a link to somewhere else.

Creating and editing needs the **Manage knowledge** permission.
