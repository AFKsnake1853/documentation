## Taking Time Off

Kith allows your staff to request time off for your HR Department to accept of deny.

Heres how to request time off in your Kith Workspace:

<Warning>
  Before you continue, make sure you have the **Take Time Off** permission in your Kith workspace.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Time Off page">
    To get to the Time Off page as a non-hr member, click Activity and then Time Off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Time Off!">
    Click the **Request Time Off** button in the top-right corner of your workspace. Fill out all the necessary details and then you are done!

    <Tip>
      We recommend asking you staff to only request LOA if it is for a week or longer.
    </Tip>

    <Frame>
      ![Time Off Middle Image](/images/Time-Off-Middle-Image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Time Off Requests

In Staff Management there is a dedicated pre-made view for managing time-off heres how to access it:

<Warning>
  Before you continue, make sure you have the **Manage Time Off** and **View Other Staff** permission in your Kith workspace.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the Staff Management page">
    Click Staff Management in the sidebar, this will bring you to the Staff Dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
