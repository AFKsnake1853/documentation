## Assigned Staff

You can see all the staff that signed up for that session and the servers they are in. You can also manually add staff to a server and assign them a role.

## Taking Attendance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Selecting a Player">
    Select an existing player or add a player by clicking the **Add** button and selecting or searching them in the popup window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Logging Attendance">
    Press the green tick to log the player as present and the red cross to log them as absent. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your session!">
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Personal Notes

You can enter Personal Notes and save them for later. For example, you could enter who failed in that Session to log later.
